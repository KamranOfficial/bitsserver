Summary — we use a small number of essential, analytics, and functional cookies to run our Site and understand how it's used. You can manage or disable non-essential cookies at any time through your browser settings.

01 What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember information about your visit, such as your preferences and login state, making your next visit easier and the site more useful.

02 How We Use Cookies

BitsServer IT Lab uses cookies to operate our Site reliably, remember your preferences (such as dark/light mode), understand how visitors interact with our content, and, where applicable, measure the effectiveness of our marketing.

03 Types of Cookies We Use

We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Essential cookies — required for core Site functionality, such as navigation and security; these cannot be disabled
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve performance and content
  • Functional cookies — remember choices you make, such as display preferences, to provide a more personalized experience
  • Marketing cookies — used, where enabled, to measure the effectiveness of our outreach and campaigns

04 Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our Site may be set by third-party services we use, such as analytics or embedded content providers. These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies, which we encourage you to review.

05 Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Most browsers let you control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting them. Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of our Site. You can typically manage cookie preferences from your browser's privacy or settings menu.

06 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies we use or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
