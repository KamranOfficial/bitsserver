01 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how BitsServer IT Lab ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information when you visit our website ("Site") or use our AI, cloud, hosting, development, cybersecurity, or consulting services (collectively, the "Services"). By using the Site or Services, you consent to the practices described here.

02 Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact details you provide via forms, email, or phone, such as your name, email address, and phone number
  • Project information shared during onboarding or consultations, such as business requirements and technical specifications
  • Billing information needed to invoice and process payments for services
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, collected automatically via cookies and similar technologies
  • Account credentials for hosted environments, dashboards, or client portals

03 How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Deliver, maintain, and improve our Services
  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support
  • Process payments and send invoices
  • Send service-related updates, such as maintenance notices or security alerts
  • Analyze Site usage to improve performance and content
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements

04 Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to operate the Site, remember preferences, and understand how visitors use our content. For full details on the types of cookies we use and how to manage them, see our Cookie Policy.

05 Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Site or deliver Services (e.g. cloud hosting, payment processors, analytics providers), bound by confidentiality obligations
  • Professional advisors, such as accountants or legal counsel, where necessary
  • Authorities, where required by law, regulation, or valid legal process

06 Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures — including encryption, access controls, and monitoring — to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

07 Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, including satisfying legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

08 Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to certain processing or request a copy of your data in a portable format. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

09 Children's Privacy

Our Site and Services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

10 International Data Transfers

As we work with clients and infrastructure providers globally, your information may be processed or stored in countries other than your own. Where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to ensure your information receives an adequate level of protection.

11 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Site or Services after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
