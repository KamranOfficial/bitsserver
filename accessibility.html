Summary — we aim to meet recognized web accessibility standards and welcome feedback on any part of the Site that's difficult to use.

01 Our Commitment

BitsServer IT Lab is committed to ensuring our website is accessible to people of all abilities. We continually work to improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards where practical.

02 Standards We Follow

We aim to align with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, which help make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, and neurological impairments.

03 Accessibility Features

Our Site includes features such as:

  • Keyboard-navigable menus and interactive elements
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and background, including in dark and light mode
  • Descriptive alt text for meaningful images
  • Consistent, predictable navigation and page structure
  • Readable typography with scalable text

04 Known Limitations

Despite our efforts, some parts of the Site may not yet be fully accessible. We are actively working to identify and fix these issues. If you encounter a barrier, please let us know so we can address it.

05 Feedback & Assistance

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our Site. If you experience difficulty accessing any part of it, or need information in an alternative format, please contact us and we'll do our best to assist.

06 Continuous Improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We periodically review our Site and update it as standards evolve and as we receive feedback from users.
